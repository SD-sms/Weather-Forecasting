# 🌦️ EcoWeather Insight – Weather Forecasting & Carbon Footprint Awareness Platform

**A Unified Platform for Weather Forecasting and Environmental Awareness**

EcoWeather Insight is a web-based platform that combines real-time weather forecasting with carbon footprint calculation and personalized eco-friendly recommendations.

The project aims to make environmental awareness a part of everyday life by helping users understand both weather conditions and the environmental impact of their daily activities through a single, simple interface.

---

## 🌍 About the Project

People regularly check weather forecasts to plan their daily activities, but very few understand how their everyday lifestyle contributes to carbon emissions.

Weather forecasting applications and carbon footprint calculators generally operate separately, making it difficult for users to connect their daily activities with their environmental impact.

**EcoWeather Insight bridges this gap** by bringing weather information and carbon footprint awareness together in one platform.

### 🎯 Objectives

- Provide real-time weather information for better daily planning.
- Help users calculate and understand their carbon footprint.
- Generate personalized eco-friendly recommendations.
- Promote sustainable lifestyle choices.
- Make climate awareness simple and accessible to everyone.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Weather Information:** Users enter or select a location to retrieve weather information through a weather API.
2. **Carbon Footprint Calculation:** Users provide information about their daily activities.
3. **Footprint Analysis:** The application processes the inputs using its carbon calculation logic.
4. **Eco Recommendations:** The platform generates suggestions to help users reduce their environmental impact.
5. **Environmental Awareness:** Users can use the combined insights to make more informed everyday decisions.
